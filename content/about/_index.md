GliMR is the Glioma and Brain Tumour MR Imaging Working Group of the European Society of Magnetic Resonance in Medicine and Biology (ESMRMB). Our objective is to ensure rapid translation of advances in current and novel neuroimaging techniques from research to clinical practice in glioma imaging diagnostics, including best practices in translational research and international data sharing. To reach this objective, our working group has several task forces that each have their own focus, from advanced MRI and AI in glioma to data sharing and educational activities. 
<br />
<br />
The GliMR Working Group builds on the experience and network generated by the EU COST Action Glioma MR Imaging 2.0. This was a COST Action funded from 2019 to 2024 that focused on building a network and facilitating networking activities for key stakeholders in glioma diagnostics, including researchers, clinicians, patients and their caregivers, and industry partners. Outputs of GliMR include several scientific meetings, two training schools, a MAGMA special issue on advanced MRI for glioma diagnostics, and multiple reviews and position statements. In addition, a video on the use of medical data and how this data is shared according to EU GDPR data regulations was made and translated into 17 languages and is freely available.
