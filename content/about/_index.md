<p>GliMR (Glioma and Brain Tumour MR Imaging) is the ESMRMB Working Group dedicated to advancing MRI in glioma and brain tumour research and clinical care. Through international collaboration, education, harmonisation and innovation, GliMR supports the development and clinical translation of imaging approaches that improve the understanding, diagnosis and management of brain tumours.</p>
