<p>
The Clinical Implementation Task Force promotes the translation of advanced MRI methods and imaging biomarkers into clinical practice for patients with glioma and brain tumours. By bringing together researchers, clinicians and imaging experts, the task force supports evidence-based implementation across healthcare settings.</p>

<p>Through position statements, consensus-building activities and guideline development, the task force aims to facilitate the safe, effective and harmonised use of advanced imaging techniques in routine clinical care.</p>
