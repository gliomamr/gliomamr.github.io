<p>
The Science Communication Task Force promotes the activities and achievements
of the GliMR community through digital communication, outreach initiatives
and educational content.
</p>

<p>
By maintaining GliMR's online presence, supporting webinar dissemination,
and developing community-focused communication materials, the task force
helps connect researchers, clinicians and stakeholders across Europe.
</p>
